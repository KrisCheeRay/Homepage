- **Authors:** Ruixing Jin, Chen Pengyu, Rui CAI, Runyi Zhao, Zhizheng Wu, and Guiliang Liu.
- **Status:** Under review at CVPR 2027.
- Developed hierarchical annotation and data-processing workflows for long-horizon robotic manipulation demonstrations.
- Integrated and validated a vision-language-action training pipeline across simulation and real-world evaluation settings.
- Built cross-benchmark data and evaluation workflows, including preprocessing, view-ordering, checkpoint, and control-interface analysis.
- Contributed to experimental analysis, related-work synthesis, figure development, citation verification, and manuscript preparation.
