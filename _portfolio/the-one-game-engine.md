Developed in C++ with SFML. My primary responsibilities were sound design, UI/UX design, packaging the complete program, and preparing the release.

The project includes a custom game loop, polymorphic entity system, scene management, state-machine animation, elemental debuffs, AABB collision detection, enemy and NPC systems, and nine levels across three elemental themes.
